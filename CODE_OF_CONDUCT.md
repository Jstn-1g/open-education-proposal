# A community where criticism is useful

These rules apply to the project repository and other official project spaces. **Response owner: Jstn-1g.** Send conduct concerns privately to [jstn0513@gmail.com](mailto:jstn0513@gmail.com). Do not include sensitive details in a public issue.

Be considerate, specific, and willing to correct mistakes. Critique ideas and evidence without attacking people. Respect different experiences, access needs, identities, and levels of technical knowledge. Give credit and make space for good-faith disagreement.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, intimidation, unwanted contact, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not post child or learner records. Participation in this edition is for adults.

Jstn-1g considers the conduct and context, protects report details as far as practicable, and uses proportionate action: a request to change behavior, content removal, a participation restriction, or removal from the community. Responders disclose conflicts and explain decisions to affected people where doing so does not expose private information.

A contributor may ask for reconsideration through the same private address. With one initial maintainer, independent appeal is not guaranteed. Conflicted cases need a willing independent reviewer and the reporter's consent before sharing private details, or a pause in the affected activity. No guaranteed response time or absolute confidentiality is promised. Send only the information needed to explain the concern; no identity documents or health details are required.
