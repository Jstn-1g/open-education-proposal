# A community where criticism is useful

These are candidate conduct rules. They take effect only when adopted with a named response owner and a working private reporting and appeal route.

Be considerate, specific, and willing to correct mistakes. Critique ideas and evidence without attacking people. Respect different experiences, access needs, identities, and levels of technical knowledge. Give credit and make space for good-faith disagreement.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, intimidation, unwanted contact, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not post child or learner records. These expectations would apply in project repositories and other official project spaces.

Responders would consider the conduct and context, protect report details as far as practicable, and use proportionate action: a request to change behavior, content removal, a participation restriction, or removal from the community. They must disclose conflicts and explain decisions to affected people where doing so does not expose private information.

A contributor may ask for reconsideration. If only one maintainer is available, independent appeal is not guaranteed; disclose that limit. Conflicted cases need a willing independent reviewer and the reporter's consent before sharing private details, or a pause in the affected activity. No response time or confidentiality guarantee is promised by this draft. **Before intake opens, publish the actual response owner, private reporting route, review capacity, and limits on appeals.**
