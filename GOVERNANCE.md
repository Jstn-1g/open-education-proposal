# How this community makes decisions

**Accountable owner and initial maintainer: Jstn-1g.** These rules cover community v0.1.0 at [Jstn-1g/open-education-proposal](https://github.com/Jstn-1g/open-education-proposal).

Use [repository issues](https://github.com/Jstn-1g/open-education-proposal/issues) for ordinary questions and corrections. Send private conduct or security concerns to [jstn0513@gmail.com](mailto:jstn0513@gmail.com). Jstn-1g handles those reports; there is no guaranteed response time or continuous monitoring.

## Decisions and responsibility

Jstn-1g is responsible for routine documentation and website decisions, review of changes, publication, and credit. Substantive decisions record their reasons and known limits. Use reviewed pull requests and the repository checks for changes.

The owner may use GPT-6 to draft code, documentation, tests, and triage notes, and to challenge proposed changes. GPT-6 is a development assistant, not a legal co-maintainer or accountable decision maker. Agreement between AI reviews does not establish correctness or independent approval.

### Routine maintenance under owner authorization

Jstn-1g remains the accountable human owner. Under a recorded standing authorization, an AI assistant may prepare, review, merge, and deploy routine corrections to this existing static website and its tooling. Routine means repairing a broken link, typo, layout or keyboard-interaction defect, or implementing a tested bug fix that preserves approved meaning, data boundaries, and existing capabilities. This authorization does not imply that a human inspected each change.

This lane excludes accepting outside contributions; adding a resource or feature; changing educational claims, assessment interpretations, or specialist-review status; and changing scope, governance, licenses, attribution, reporting policies, data handling, dependencies, workflow definitions or execution permissions, hosting configuration, or file-write boundaries. Spending, account creation, outreach, child-facing work, and learner data are also excluded. These require their own owner decision; substantive educational, safeguarding, and accessibility claims still need relevant qualified review or must remain unresolved.

Every delegated correction needs a scoped pull request identifying the applicable authorization, affected behavior, actual AI/human review, tests and limitations. Review the exact diff and actual required check results; do not bypass protections, dismiss findings to obtain passing checks, or claim human or specialist approval without evidence. Running the unchanged, approved deployment workflow is allowed for a checked correction in this lane; deployment remains a separate deliberate action on that commit, followed by live verification. A failing required check blocks merging and deployment, not in-scope investigation or repair. If a change or its remediation does not clearly fit this lane, or a material risk remains unresolved, pause for the owner. Jstn-1g may suspend or revoke this lane at any time.

Outside this narrow lane, a human reviews the work and decides whether to accept it. The policy amendment itself requires an explicit owner decision; it cannot authorize itself.

Substantive pedagogy, assessment, safeguarding, and accessibility claims need review by people with relevant competence. If that review is unavailable, keep the claim unresolved or narrow it. Routine corrections and website improvements can still proceed. Automated checks do not confer educational or professional approval.

Anyone participating may challenge a proposal with reasons or evidence. A disagreement can remain visible without being treated as misconduct. Reviewers disclose relevant conflicts and step back from decisions where they cannot review fairly.

Changes to scope, licenses, participation rules, or maintainer authority need a short written proposal and a recorded owner decision. The selected licenses are Apache-2.0 for software and CC BY 4.0 for content, as described in the [license plan](LICENSE-PLAN.md). Community feedback does not authorize a child-facing product or research.

## Corrections and continuity

Sensitive reports need human handling; they must not be copied into public issues or AI tools. A contributor may request reconsideration with new evidence or reasons. A single maintainer cannot promise an independent appeal. When Jstn-1g has a conflict or a complaint concerns their conduct, seek a willing independent reviewer with the reporter's consent before sharing private details. If a fair review is unavailable, pause the affected decision or participation rather than imply independence.

Jstn-1g can pause contributions, correct or remove harmful material, and suspend publication when necessary. Record the reason without exposing sensitive information. Before a maintainer leaves, assign their responsibilities with the successor's consent or pause the affected work.

The [maintenance routine](MAINTENANCE.md) describes checks, security review, and follow-through. Listing a contact address does not enable automated inbox access; email handling remains a human responsibility.
