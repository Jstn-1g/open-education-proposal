# Sources and the limits of our claims

This is a starting reading list, not a systematic literature review. Source identities and the narrow notes below were checked on **4 September 2026** against publisher pages, primary texts, official guidance, or the original abstract as specified. No source validates this proposal or a product we have not tested.

## Learning and evidence

- [Bastani et al. (2025), *Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning: Evidence from high school mathematics*](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122) — The [primary full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12232635/) confirms a randomized study at one high school in Turkey, with assisted practice and subsequent unassisted exams. These were short-term outcomes: the study does not establish long-term retention, effects in younger children, or results for every AI design. The [August 2025 correction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12403119/) concerns an author's affiliation, not the reported findings. Title, authors, DOI, methods, limitations, and correction checked.
- [Karpicke, Blunt, and Smith (2016), *Retrieval-Based Learning: Positive Effects of Retrieval Practice in Elementary School Children*](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2016.00350) — The [publisher full text](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2016.00350/full) describes three experiments using word lists and cued retrieval with elementary-school children. No feedback was provided during retrieval. This does not validate every retrieval-plus-feedback activity or a mathematics assessment. Title, authors, DOI, and relevant methods checked.
- [Barnett and Ceci (2002), *When and where do we apply what we learn? A taxonomy for far transfer*](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.128.4.612) — The [original abstract and citation record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12081085/) confirm a framework distinguishing dimensions of transfer. Our caution about new-looking questions is a design inference, not a validated assessment rule. Title, authors, DOI, and abstract checked; full text was not reviewed in this pass.
- [IES / What Works Clearinghouse (2007), *Organizing Instruction and Study to Improve Student Learning*](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1) — This practice guide, led by Harold Pashler, includes spacing, worked examples, retrieval through quizzing, and explanatory questions. Its recommendations carry different evidence ratings. The official title, date, panel, and recommendation summaries were checked; the guide does not validate this project's reporting framework.

Our separation of instructional help and later evidence is a proposed design and reporting rule. The distinction between access support and help depends on the target skill and needs specialist review.

## Rights, access, and openness

- [UNICEF, *Guidance on AI and children*, version 3.0 (December 2025)](https://www.unicef.org/innocenti/reports/policy-guidance-ai-children) — The official page confirms recommendations covering child rights, privacy, inclusion, and accountability. Guidance is not a product evaluation, a guarantee of safety, or a substitute for applicable law. Title, version, date, and summary checked; this is not a clause-by-clause review of the guidance.
- [W3C, *Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2*](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) — The Recommendation describes testable criteria and a combination of automated testing and human evaluation. It does not cover every user's needs. Title, version, and relevant introductory and conformance text checked; no conformance finding about our site follows.
- [Open Source Initiative, *The Open Source Definition*](https://opensource.org/osd) — The official definition requires qualifying distribution terms, not merely access to source code. Title and criteria checked. This concerns software; the project's content license is a separate decision.
- [Creative Commons, *Attribution 4.0 International*](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) — The official deed summarizes reuse and attribution terms and explicitly distinguishes itself from the legal code. Title and deed checked in the source review; this was not legal advice. The owner has since selected CC BY 4.0 for project content; see [licenses and attribution](../LICENSES.md) and the included legal text for the actual terms.

## Link access

## Improve a source note

Identify the exact claim and primary source, distinguish findings from our interpretation, state the population and relevant limits, and propose a correction when needed. Keep a short record of substantive corrections in the resulting issue or pull request. The [first evidence task](FIRST-CONTRIBUTIONS.md#2-check-one-evidence-claim) provides a bounded starting point.
