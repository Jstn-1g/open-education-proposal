Question and improve.

Contribute examples, corrections, accessible design, and tests. Decisions should explain their reasons and credit the work.

02 / A longer-term ambition

A public core.
No model required.

Future learning materials and tools should offer a complete useful path without requiring a proprietary AI service. Essential capabilities and rights should not become premium features.

That ambition is not delivered by this first release. Child-facing tools, classroom use, data collection, and research would each need their own evidence and review before development or use.

A real tradeoff: permissive licenses can help schools and communities adapt the work widely, but do not require every downstream modification to be shared. We have selected broad reuse for the first edition and made that choice explicit.
