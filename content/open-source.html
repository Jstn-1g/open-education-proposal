Read and inspect.

See the proposal, its limits, and the sources behind its claims. Read the proposal and static explanations without scripts or accounts. Interactive play needs JavaScript, but no proprietary AI model.

Adapt and share.

The selected licenses support reuse and adaptation with their required notices and attribution. Names and marks are separate.

Question and improve.

Contribute examples, corrections, accessible design, and tests. Decisions should explain their reasons and credit the work.

02 / Something to work on

Six cases.
One useful question.

A short, editable draft explores help and access through six fictional situations: reading aloud, speech-to-text, calculators, supplied reasoning, and collaboration. Pick one case and challenge an assumption.

Adult discussion draft · revision 0.1. Not yet specialist-reviewed. This is not a classroom resource, assessment, or rule for granting or removing support. It includes sources, uncertainties, and CC BY 4.0 attribution.

Reading and downloading need no account or AI service; commenting needs a GitHub account. The download is plain text you can edit in a text editor. A complete learning path without a proprietary model remains a future ambition, not something this release delivers.

A real tradeoff: permissive licenses can help schools and communities adapt the work widely, but do not require every downstream modification to be shared. We have selected broad reuse for the first edition and made that choice explicit.
