01 / Three places to begin

Choose your
first contribution.

TASK 01
Educators & access specialists

Make help versus access practical.

Describe six fictional situations: the target skill, support used, whether it supplies the skill, and what is uncertain. Compare reading aloud for mathematics with reading aloud for word decoding.

Ready for review when

  • All six cases name the skill first.
  • At least one needs specialist judgment.
  • Assistive support is not automatically excluded.
  • Two relevant sources and their limits are linked.
TASK 02
Researchers & careful readers

Check one evidence claim.

Write a one-page note on why successful assisted work need not establish independent learning. Start with the Bastani study and its limits.

Ready for review when

  • The primary paper and date are verified.
  • Population and comparison are described.
  • Limits for younger learners and this project are clear.
  • A correction is proposed where needed.
TASK 03
Access reviewers, designers & builders

Remove one reading barrier.

Review the website or contributor documents using keyboard navigation, zoom, assistive technology, or accessible language expertise. Report one reproducible barrier and a focused remedy.

Ready for review when

  • The page, setup, and steps are specific.
  • Expected and observed behavior are clear.
  • The remedy is checked in the same conditions.
  • No whole-site conformance claim is implied.

02 / How to contribute

Small changes.
Thoughtful review.

  1. Choose a task. Read the contribution guide and check existing issues.
  2. Share your idea. Open an issue with the problem, proposed change, sources, and known limits. Pasted text is welcome; a pull request is optional.
  3. Review it together. Jstn-1g reviews changes, records the reason for a decision, and credits the work. This is best-effort maintenance, not an instant-response service.

Disclose any AI assistance. You remain responsible for accuracy and permission to share your work. The code of conduct applies; report sensitive concerns privately.
