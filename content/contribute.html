02 / How to contribute

Small changes.
Thoughtful review.

  1. Choose a task. Open a discussion above, or use the issue chooser for a different topic.
  2. Leave a comment. Ask one question or describe the problem. Sources are needed for evidence claims, not to report a barrier. A proposed fix and a pull request are optional.
  3. Review it together. Jstn-1g records a decision and credits the work. Specialist questions need qualified reviewers; AI assistance does not replace them. Responses are best effort.

See the contribution guide. Disclose material AI assistance and share only work you have permission to share. The code of conduct applies; report sensitive concerns privately.
