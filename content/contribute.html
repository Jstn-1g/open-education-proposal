01 / Three places to begin

Choose your
first contribution.

TASK 01
Educators & access specialists

Make help versus access practical.

Describe six fictional situations: the target skill, support used, whether it supplies the skill, and what is uncertain. Compare reading aloud for mathematics with reading aloud for word decoding.

Ready for review when

  • All six cases name the skill first.
  • At least one needs specialist judgment.
  • Assistive support is not automatically excluded.
  • Two relevant sources and their limits are linked.
TASK 02
Researchers & careful readers

Check one evidence claim.

Write a one-page note on why successful assisted work need not establish independent learning. Start with the Bastani study and its limits.

Ready for review when

  • The primary paper and date are verified.
  • Population and comparison are described.
  • Limits for younger learners and this project are clear.
  • A correction is proposed where needed.
TASK 03
Readers, access reviewers & builders

Report one reading barrier.

Tell us what made the website or contributor documents difficult to use or understand. You do not need a fix, a reproducible example, or proof of disability. A remedy is welcome, not required.

A useful report describes

  • The page and what you were trying to do.
  • What got in the way and what you expected.
  • Setup or steps you can share, if helpful.

The maintainer records a response or reason to defer. If a fix is made, recheck it in the same conditions where possible; unresolved barriers stay visible.

02 / How to contribute

Small changes.
Thoughtful review.

  1. Choose a task. Read the contribution guide and check existing issues.
  2. Share your idea. Describe the problem and any proposed change, sources, or limits you can share. Start with one example or question; pasted text is welcome and a pull request is optional.
  3. Review it together. Jstn-1g reviews changes, records the reason for a decision, and credits the work. Specialist questions need qualified reviewers; AI assistance does not replace them. This is best-effort maintenance, not an instant-response service.

Disclose material AI assistance. You remain responsible for accuracy and permission to share your work. The code of conduct applies; report sensitive concerns privately.
