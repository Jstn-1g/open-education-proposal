C1 / Mathematics

Compare two fractions.

The intended skill
Explain which of two fractions is greater. Decoding written words is not the target.

The support
A prompt is read aloud without explaining the comparison or emphasizing an answer.

What can this response show?

Our provisional interpretation
Access support may preserve the mathematical task. The response still needs its own interpretation.

What needs scrutiny
Could wording, emphasis, or a description of a diagram supply the comparison strategy? Subject and access reviewers must examine the exact task.

C2 / Reading

Decode a printed word.

The intended skill
Decode an unfamiliar printed word.

The support
The target word is spoken aloud before the response.

What can this response show?

Our provisional interpretation
The support supplies the pronunciation being examined. Describe supported word work, not independent print decoding.

What needs scrutiny
The same read-aloud support has a different role than in C1. Keep necessary support; do not turn this example into an instruction to remove it.
