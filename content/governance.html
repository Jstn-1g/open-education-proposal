02 / How we intend to work

People decide.
Reasons stay visible.

Jstn-1g is responsible for reviewing changes, documenting decisions, and crediting contributions. A disagreement should receive a reasoned answer; a conflict of interest should be disclosed and referred to another reviewer where possible.

GPT-6 assists code, documentation, tests, and review. It is not an accountable maintainer or an independent subject specialist. AI agreement does not establish correctness, and no continuous response service is promised.

Child-facing implementation, participant research, learner records, AI companions, and robot tutors are outside this community edition.

Sources and limits

The evidence-reporting rule is our proposal. These sources provide context, not validation or endorsement of the project.

  • Bastani et al. (2025): Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning — a randomized study at one Turkish high school comparing assisted mathematics practice and subsequent unassisted exams. Results depend on the design and setting; these short-term outcomes do not establish long-term retention or effects for all AI tools.
  • IES: Organizing Instruction and Study to Improve Student Learning (2007) — a practice guide covering spacing, retrieval, and related learning strategies, with differing evidence ratings; it does not validate our reporting framework.
  • W3C: Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2 — the accessibility target for this website; a target is not a conformance finding.
  • UNICEF: Guidance on AI and children, version 3.0 (December 2025) — context for child rights and accountability; not approval for a child-facing system.

Privacy

This static website has no forms, accounts, client scripts, analytics, or project-set cookies. GitHub Pages is the selected public host. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including visitors who are not signed in; see GitHub Pages hosting information and its privacy statement. GitHub also processes your participation on its repository. Public contributions are visible to others.

Email reports go to Jstn-1g’s Gmail inbox, not a website form. Share only what is needed. No AI inbox access, automated report processing, or continuous monitoring is configured by this project.

Corrections and private concerns

For non-sensitive corrections, use a GitHub issue. For conduct or security concerns, email jstn0513@gmail.com, received by Jstn-1g. Never post private reports, credentials, or children’s information publicly. Responses are best effort; no response-time guarantee or emergency service is offered.
