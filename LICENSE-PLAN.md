## The tradeoff

This pair favors broad reuse, including commercial reuse. It does **not** require every downstream improvement to be shared back. A share-alike approach could require reciprocity but would change reuse and compatibility tradeoffs. The owner selected broad reuse for this first public proposal, while inviting improvements through the contribution process.

Safeguarding commitments describe this project's own activities. They are not additional field-of-use restrictions attached to the open licenses. A person can reuse licensed material without implying our endorsement.
